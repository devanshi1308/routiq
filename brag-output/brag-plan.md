# Brag Plan: Routiq

## What is this app?
Routiq is an Indian ride-hailing app (autos, bikes, cabs) where you track the ride live, share it with people you trust, and get cheeky reminders instead of boring ones.

## The angle
Every ride app reminds you in corporate-speak. Routiq's own notification copy is the joke ("auto bhaiya is bored"), and then the video proves the app is serious where it counts: live tracking, women-first safety, safe-arrival messages. Funny hook, trustworthy middle.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A lock-screen notification drops in on black: "auto bhaiya is bored 🛺 — he's been parked for ages. be kind, book a ride?" (real Routiq notification copy).

## Key moments (the middle)
- Choose a ride: Auto ₹71.70, Routiq Go AC ₹116.00 (Fastest), Bike ₹38.90 (Cheapest), then "Searching 2 services: Auto + Bike. The first driver to accept takes your ride."
- Live ride: the auto drives the route, one progress bar with a percentage badge, "Driver arrives in 3 min" counting down to "Arrived".
- Safety, on your terms: Women Rider Preferences switch turns on; share link "Anyone with the link follows your ride live"; message "Asha reached home safely 🏠".

## Outro / punchline
Logo, "Your ride. Your people." and a small kicker: "Split the fare, not the friendship 🤝" (also real Routiq notification copy).

## User flow worth showing
Entry: open Routiq. Key action: choose a ride and add a second service. Result: watch the driver arrive on the live bar, then share the trip and send "reached home safely".

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: cheeky Hinglish ride app that is serious about safety
- Interpretation: playful opening, clean white product scenes, calm dark safety scene, confident logo finish. No generic SaaS words.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #000000 (hook and outro), #FFFFFF / #F3F3F3 (product scenes), #0B0B0B (safety)
- Accent: #05944F (green), #276EF1 (blue)
- Text: #000000 on light, #FFFFFF on dark
- Display font: Plus Jakarta Sans (ExtraBold, Bold, Medium)
- Body font: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Strongest visual element: the green auto, the single progress bar with the moving percentage badge, the Routiq pin logo

## Share copy (draft)
Routiq: book an auto, watch it arrive on one live bar, and tell your people you got home safe. Built for Mumbai.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse motion-matched accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 (Sascha Ende, CC BY 4.0), about 110 BPM
- Music treatment: starts at 0, volume about 0.5, fade out over the last 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: beat grid every ~0.55s; strong cues at 8.74s (scene 3), 13.11s (scene 4), 17.47s (logo). Scene cuts land on or near these.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (keep the UI cards steady)
- SFX posture: sparse; a click on the second-service chip, a switch on the toggle, a drop on the logo
- Restraint rule: no stinger on every card

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3.3s
Black. Notification card slides in from the top, holds long enough to read.
Audio intent: music in, one soft click as the card lands.
Transition mood: hard cut on beat at 3.27s → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Pick a ride — 5.5s (3.27 to 8.74)
White. "Pick a ride." with a ride list (Auto, Go AC, Bike). Second-service card slides up: "Searching 2 services".
Sequential/interaction: three rows arrive one by one, then the "+ Bike" chip is tapped and the card appears.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Watch it come to you — 4.4s (8.74 to 13.11)
Light grey. Route with the auto driving it, one progress bar with a moving percentage badge, headline counting 3 min, 2 min, 1 min, Arrived.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Safe, on your terms — 4.4s (13.11 to 17.47)
Dark. Three cards one by one: Women Rider switch turns on, live share link, safe-arrival message.
Transition mood: dramatic → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 2.5s (17.47 to 20)
Black. Logo hits on the strong cue, "Your ride. Your people.", kicker line, music fades.
