# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Routiq

## Objective
Create a short launch-style brag video for Routiq, an Indian ride-hailing app.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape, 1920x1080
- Duration: 20 seconds

## Source Material
- Project root: /Users/devanshi/Downloads/uber
- Primary files read: README, app screens (choose-ride, trip, safety, share-trip, notifications), lib/notify.ts, tracker page
- Product name: Routiq
- Tagline / strongest claim: Your ride. Your people.
- Key UI or visual moment to recreate: ride list, "Searching 2 services" card, single live progress bar with percentage badge, safe-arrival message
- Copy that must appear verbatim:
  - auto bhaiya is bored 🛺 / he's been parked for ages. be kind, book a ride?
  - Searching 2 services / The first driver to accept takes your ride.
  - Asha reached home safely 🏠
  - Split the fare, not the friendship 🤝

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: default; direction: cheeky Hinglish ride app that is serious about safety
- Angle: funny notification hook, then proof it is serious where it counts
- Hook: lock-screen notification on black
- Outro: logo, "Your ride. Your people."
- Avoid: generic SaaS language, abstract filler, any real names or numbers of people (Asha and Prakash J. are fictional)

## Visual Identity
- Background: #000 / #fff / #f3f3f3 / #0b0b0b; accent #05944F and #276EF1
- Display font: Plus Jakarta Sans (local TTF)
